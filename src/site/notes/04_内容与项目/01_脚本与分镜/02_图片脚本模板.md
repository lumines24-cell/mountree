# 图片生成模板

## 静态图 提示词 模板

```text
[ASPECT RATIO]. [PERSONA OR HANDS-ONLY] in [SCENE / SURFACE]. [ONE PRODUCT MOMENT]. [CAMERA + COMPOSITION]. [LIGHT / ART DIRECTION]. Natural restrained premium lifestyle aesthetic with clear breathing room around the product.

[PRODUCT REFERENCE]
[CONSTRAINTS / NEGATIVE]
```

## 示例：通勤门厅上脚图

```text
4:5 vertical image. A poised professional woman in an ivory knit top and tailored black trousers stands in a bright apartment entryway with light oak flooring, a coat rack and a structured tote. One shoe is set slightly forward, showing the exact toe shape and the transition from trouser hem to shoe silhouette. Calf-to-floor composition, natural scale, soft morning window light, warm white surfaces and a restrained pine-green accent. Natural restrained premium lifestyle aesthetic with generous breathing room.

Use the attached Mountree shoe reference as the authoritative visual source. Preserve the exact silhouette, toe shape, heel height, sole pattern, color and material finish.
Negative: generated text, readable logos, watermark, product drift, warped shoes, warped feet, extra fingers, extra limbs, impossible anatomy.
```
