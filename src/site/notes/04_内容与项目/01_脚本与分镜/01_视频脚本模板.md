## AI 视频 提示词 模板

```text
Vertical 9:16, 5–8 second single continuous shot. [PERSONA] wearing [OUTFIT] is in [SCENE + TIME]. [OPENING HOOK / ACTION]. Then she [ONE PRIMARY ACTION], creating [ONE PRODUCT MOMENT]. [CAMERA + COMPOSITION]. [LIGHT / ART DIRECTION]. [SOUND]. [OPTIONAL SHORT SPOKEN LINE].

[PRODUCT REFERENCE]
[CONSTRAINTS / NEGATIVE]
```

## 示例：电梯对镜

```text
Vertical 9:16, 5–8 second single continuous shot. A poised professional woman in an ivory knit top, tailored black trousers and a structured tote stands in a clean residential elevator with brushed metal walls. She checks the outfit silhouette and shifts her weight once, then angles one toe toward the mirror so the exact shoe profile fills the lower third of the frame. Ordinary iPhone mirror perspective with subtle hand-held bounce. Practical overhead lighting softened by pale interior surfaces; quiet elevator room tone. "This is my meeting-day pair."

Use the attached Mountree shoe reference as the authoritative visual source. Preserve the exact silhouette, toe shape, heel height, sole pattern, color and material finish.
Negative: generated text, readable logos, watermark, product drift, warped shoes, warped feet, extra fingers, extra limbs, impossible anatomy, impossible gait, jump cuts.
```
