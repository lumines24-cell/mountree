## 音频路线变量

> 先根据生成界面确认是否启用支持原生音频的模型；Prompt 只能描述声音意图，不能开启模型未提供的音频能力。原生音频不稳定时，画面保留环境声意图，口播与音乐在后期完成。

```yaml
id: audio_native_ambient_only
type: audio_route
text: Generate quiet apartment room tone and soft natural footsteps only. No music and no dialogue.
fit: [flow_native_audio, seedance_audio]
status: optional
```

```yaml
id: audio_native_one_line
type: audio_route
text: The woman speaks one short line clearly to camera in a natural conversational voice; quiet apartment room tone continues underneath. No music.
fit: [flow_native_audio, seedance_audio]
status: experimental
```

```yaml
id: audio_post_dialogue
type: audio_route
text: Generate no intelligible dialogue. Leave clean room tone and a one-second pause for post-production voiceover and captions.
fit: [all]
status: recommended_fallback
```

```yaml
id: dialogue_speaker_to_camera
type: dialogue_direction
text: The on-screen woman speaks directly to camera once; keep her face visible, head movement minimal, and the phone framing stable during the line.
fit: [ugc, handheld, situational]
status: required_for_native_lipsync
```

```yaml
id: dialogue_meeting_day_pair
type: spoken_line
text: This is my meeting-day pair.
fit: [ugc]
status: native_audio_or_post
```
