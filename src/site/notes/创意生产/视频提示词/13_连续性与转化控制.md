# 连续性与转化控制

> 本模块用于 5–10 秒单镜头的原生 UGC。它只控制产品可读性、生成连续性与已核验的转化信息；不替代产品事实、合规或平台规则。

## 调用原则

- 先填写 `product_state_*`，再选本模块的连续性与可见性变量。
- 单镜头只保留一个主动作、一种钩子和一个 CTA；台词优先后期，除非已验证原生口型和音频。
- 促销、免邮与库存信息必须存在已审批活动；没有就不生成、不口播。产品话术仅描述参考图中可见的细节，不使用功效或性能表述。

## 钩子机制

```yaml
id: hook_sensory_secret
type: hook_pattern
text: Open with one quiet sensory cue and a brief invitation to notice a verified visible detail; keep the product readable in the first second.
fit: [ugc, handheld]
goal: [first_second_hold, product_attention]
status: post_or_verified_lipsync
```

```yaml
id: hook_context_contrast
type: hook_pattern
text: Place one ordinary Mountree workday product moment in a calm, unexpected but plausible setting; the contrast must never obscure the shoe or contradict the brand's restrained professional tone.
fit: [ugc, situational]
goal: [pattern_interrupt, save]
status: approved_scene_only
```

```yaml
id: hook_commute_micro_problem
type: hook_pattern
text: Open with one concise workday moment, then show a verified visual detail or ordinary wearing moment; do not make product-effect or performance promises.
fit: [ugc, situational]
goal: [relevance, completion]
status: claim_checked
```

## 首帧、可见性与连续性

```yaml
id: continuity_mirror_reflection_lock
type: continuity_contract
text: From first frame to final frame, preserve one mirror plane, one reflected woman, one phone position and one matching worn pair. The phone may move subtly with the hand, but the reflection must remain physically continuous and the shoes remain readable.
fit: [mirror_selfie]
status: required
source: [mirror_route_rebuild]
```

```yaml
id: continuity_first_frame_held_product
type: first_frame_state
text: From the first frame, exactly one referenced shoe is already visible, naturally supported in the presenter's hand; no object may appear, disappear, swap or emerge from packaging during this clip.
fit: [handheld, ugc]
status: required_when_held_product
```

```yaml
id: continuity_single_take_state_lock
type: continuity_contract
text: "Use one continuous take. Keep the presenter, referenced product, wardrobe, scene and light direction consistent. Movement is slow and physically connected: no jump cuts, teleports, sudden hand changes, object resizing, object replacement or aggressive rotation."
fit: [ugc, handheld, situational]
status: required
```

```yaml
id: product_visibility_hero_face_lock
type: product_visibility_plan
text: Keep the one verified hero face of the shoe readable for most of the clip. Show the full silhouette in the first second; hands may touch a verified detail briefly but must not hide the selected hero face for longer than one second.
fit: [handheld, ugc, product_led]
status: required
input: [verified_hero_face, verified_detail_id]
```

## 十秒节拍与声音

```yaml
id: beat_10s_commute_time_pause
type: beat_sheet
text: '0.0–1.5s: the worn pair is already readable as she walks toward camera. 1.5–3.0s: one hand raise and a subtle refraction pulse; distant commuters pause. 3.0–7.0s: she takes one ordinary step and holds a verified side profile. 7.0–10.0s: the lobby motion and ambience return as she continues walking. One continuous take, no dialogue or CTA.'
fit: [dynamic_concept, office_walk]
duration: 10
status: concept_only
```

```yaml
id: beat_10s_sensory_proof_cta
type: beat_sheet
text: '0.0–1.0s: hook and full shoe silhouette. 1.0–4.0s: one quiet product-touch action. 4.0–7.0s: hold or lightly angle one verified detail. 7.0–10.0s: one approved CTA or a clean final product hold. Keep it one take and one information unit per beat.'
fit: [ugc, handheld]
duration: 10
status: required_for_10s
```

```yaml
id: audio_asmr_soft_product_touch
type: audio_direction
text: Quiet close-mic-inspired room tone with one soft touch sound such as a light fingertip brush or gentle tap on a verified product surface. No music. Keep any spoken line brief and quieter than the product sound; if native audio is unreliable, generate room tone only and add voice in post.
fit: [ugc, handheld]
status: native_or_post_fallback
```

```yaml
id: ugc_phone_natural_restrained
type: ugc_authenticity
text: Natural vertical smartphone framing, subtle handheld micro-movement, ordinary blink rate and realistic skin texture. Keep styling clean, feminine and professional; no beauty filter, hyper-polished commercial motion or exaggerated influencer performance.

```

## 生成前检查

`产品状态已选 / 首帧可见 / 主视角已核验 / 只含一个主动作 / 节拍匹配时长 / 原生音频可用性已确认 / 产品话术仅为可见细节 / 每条促销 CTA 有活动审批 / 后期字幕与 CTA 已分离`。
