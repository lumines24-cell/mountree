# 电商产品静物 提示词

> 适用：商品页、Amazon 辅图、细节图、产品目录。所有 Prompt 后加「[[02_提示词与生成/01_图片提示词/01_按拍摄方式/00_图片提示词索引#每条 Prompt 末尾都加\|00_图片提示词索引#每条 Prompt 末尾都加]]」。

## 白底 45° 目录图

```text
A single Mountree shoe shown at a precise three-quarter front angle, centered on a pure white seamless background, full shoe visible and filling about 80 percent of the frame. Soft diffused studio key from upper left, gentle fill from camera right, small natural contact shadow directly under the sole. 50mm lens, f/8, neutral white balance, catalog-grade clarity, 1:1 composition.
```

## 侧面与鞋底边缘

```text
A precise side and outsole-edge product photograph. The shoe is tilted slightly so the side profile, heel and sole edge are clearly readable. Matte pale stone surface, soft daylight from upper left with subtle bounce fill from the right, 85mm lens, f/8, realistic grounded shadow, warm neutral palette, clean technical editorial composition, 4:5 vertical.
```

## 鞋面微距

```text
A tight macro photograph focused on [one verified visible upper detail]. Keep the material color, surface and stitching accurate. The shoe rests on warm cream linen with soft natural creases. Window light from camera-left at 45 degrees, 100mm macro lens, f/4, focus locked on the detail, natural color reproduction, restrained editorial finish, 4:5 vertical.
```

## 鞋底局部

```text
A close technical product photograph of the outsole and heel base, with the shoe resting naturally on a pale textured surface. The sole pattern is sharp and undistorted, the shoe remains grounded, soft even studio light, 85mm lens, f/8, low-contrast shadow, 4:5 vertical.
```

## 通勤胶囊平铺

```text
Overhead flat lay on warm off-white linen: one pair of shoes, a folded [blazer color] blazer, slim blank notebook, simple watch and closed ceramic coffee cup. Generous negative space, soft window light from upper left, low-contrast shadows, calm professional workday mood, muted cream, stone and moss-green palette, 4:5 vertical.
```
