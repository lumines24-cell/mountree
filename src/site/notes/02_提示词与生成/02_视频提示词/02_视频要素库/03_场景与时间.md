# 场景与时间

> 一个镜头只选一个具体地点。场景要为“上班前、通勤中、工作后、出行前”服务，不只提供漂亮背景。

## 家中准备

- `a calm apartment bedroom with oak floor and an off-white wall`
- `a bright entryway with a coat rack and a structured tote`
- `a minimalist walk-in wardrobe with natural morning light`
- `a tidy apartment entryway bench before leaving for work`

## 通勤与工作

- `a clean residential elevator with brushed metal walls`
- `a quiet office corridor after a meeting`
- `a neutral stone office restroom mirror`
- `a cafe window seat with a tote beside the chair`

## 工作以外的延展

- `a softly lit hotel hallway before dinner`
- `a hotel room while packing for a work trip`
- `a parked car before walking into an event`
- `a quiet apartment balcony in late afternoon`

## 时间与氛围池

- `soft morning window light`
- `a practical early-evening interior`
- `late afternoon with warm but realistic daylight`
- `a quiet weekday transition from work to dinner`

## 场景道具原则

- 每镜头最多 1–2 个叙事道具：托特包、笔记本、咖啡杯、衣架或旅行箱。
- 道具无可读 Logo、无品牌混淆、无不必要的桌面杂物。
