# 人物与造型

> 人物是“谁在真实地使用这双鞋”，不是泛化时尚模特。一个系列固定人物描述与造型基调，减少生成漂移。

## 人物原型池

- `a poised professional woman getting ready for a workday`
- `a woman leaving home for a client meeting`
- `a creative professional moving from desk to dinner`
- `a frequent work traveler packing for one evening plan`
- `a woman doing a calm, practical outfit check before leaving`
- `hands-only product interaction; no face shown`

## 造型池

- `an ivory knit top, tailored black trousers and a structured tote`
- `a soft moss-green blouse with a dark midi skirt`
- `a charcoal blazer, white shirt and straight-leg trousers`
- `a cream trench over a simple black work dress`
- `a neutral knit dress with a long tailored coat`
- `a crisp white shirt, dark denim and a quiet leather-free tote`

## 状态与表演限制

- `unhurried, self-assured and natural`
- `realistic skin texture, natural blink rate, no beauty filter`
- `a small genuine reaction, never an exaggerated endorsement`
- `ordinary posture and gait; no fashion-runway posing`
- `one person only, with no background crowd`
