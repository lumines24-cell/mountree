# 光线、色彩与美术

> Mountree 画面基调：松绿 / 苔色点缀、米白、橡木、自然留白；高级感来自真实材料与克制，不来自重滤镜。

## 光线池

- `soft window light with realistic exposure variation`
- `practical overhead lighting softened by pale interior surfaces`
- `late-afternoon daylight with gentle shadow edges`
- `quiet warm room light balanced with a small window source`
- `soft, even fitting-room light with no harsh glare`

## 美术与色彩池

- `ivory wall, light oak and a restrained pine-green accent`
- `warm white textiles, charcoal tailoring and muted moss details`
- `neutral stone, brushed metal and calm off-white surfaces`
- `natural restrained premium lifestyle aesthetic, never glossy`
- `clean composition with breathing room around the product`

## 排除项

- 无霓虹、无过度金色滤镜、无强烈紫蓝渐变、无“奢侈品广告棚拍”光泽。
- 画面中不出现 AI 生成的可读 Logo、价格牌、包装文案或认证章。
