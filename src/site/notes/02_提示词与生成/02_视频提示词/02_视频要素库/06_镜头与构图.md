# 镜头与构图

## 视频机位池

- `ordinary iPhone mirror perspective with subtle hand-held bounce`
- `locked ankle-height camera, no fast movement`
- `phone camera placed on a low bench, stable framing`
- `low three-quarter view, one smooth ordinary pass`
- `front-camera phone framing with casual eye-line shifts`
- `top-down phone close-up for hands-only unboxing`
- `static close-up; the subject moves through frame once`
- `top-down floor-mirror composition with the shoe reflection centered`
- `low-angle mirror selfie focused on the ankle and shoe profile`
- `low three-quarter office-lobby view, with the shoe in the lower third`
- `handheld product close-up by a window, then one small reframing`

## 图片构图池

- `full body with shoes clearly visible in the lower third`
- `calf-to-floor composition with one shoe forward`
- `three-quarter product view with generous ivory negative space`
- `top-down hands-only composition with one shoe and tissue paper`
- `side profile close-up with natural scale and no forced perspective`

## 选镜头时的判断

| 想展示 | 优先构图 |
| --- | --- |
| 整体穿搭 | 全身对镜 / 低机位三分之四 |
| 鞋型 | 小腿到脚 / 鞋级低机位 |
| 鞋底 | 稳定近景，短暂露出 |
| 包装 | 俯拍手部近景 |
| 口播 | 胸像手持，鞋只出现一次 |

## 运镜限制

```text
Natural, motivated micro camera movement only: a gentle follow, small reframing or slow push-in. No sweeping cinematic move, no extreme zoom and no jump cuts.
```
