# 声音、台词与字幕

## 声音池

- `quiet elevator room tone and subtle phone handling sound`
- `crisp tissue-paper sound, otherwise quiet room tone`
- `soft cardboard friction and natural window ambience`
- `ordinary apartment ambience with no background music`
- `soft shoe steps on a clean indoor floor`
- `subtle tabletop contact sounds; never exaggerated ASMR`

## 口播句池

- `"This is the pair I reach for."`
- `"Let me show you the detail first."`
- `"This is my easiest outfit decision."`
- `"Okay, look at this shape."`
- `"I wanted one shoe for this whole plan."`
- `"Here is the part worth seeing up close."`

## 通勤单鞋导入台词

- `"My meeting-day pair."`
- `"The shape is what got me."`
- `"A clean line changes the whole look."`
- `"Which one would you wear first?"`
- `"See the details in the link."`

## 台词规则

- 一条最多 1 句；说话和产品动作不抢同一秒。
- 价格、材料、舒适、环保和认证只使用产品事实卡中已核验的表达。
- AI 生成画面中的字幕、Logo、鞋盒文案不可直接上线；统一在后期用真实品牌资产添加。
