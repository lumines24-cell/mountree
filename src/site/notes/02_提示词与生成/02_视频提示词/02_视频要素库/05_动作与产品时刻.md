# 动作与产品时刻

> AI 视频最稳定的单位是“一个主要动作 + 一个鞋款时刻”。不要在 5–8 秒内要求试穿、走路、转身、口播和开箱全部发生。

## 主动作池

- `checks the outfit silhouette and shifts her weight once`
- `takes exactly three relaxed steps toward the camera`
- `slips one foot into the shoe, then holds the profile still`
- `turns one foot slowly to reveal the side profile`
- `lifts one shoe into a pool of window light`
- `folds the tissue paper back to reveal one shoe`
- `holds the shoe at chest height and rotates it once`
- `places one heel down gently after a small forward step`

## 产品时刻池

- `the toe shape fills the lower third of the frame`
- `one ankle turns slightly to reveal the side profile`
- `the transition from trouser hem to shoe silhouette`
- `the shoe is held still in natural window light`
- `the open box reveals one shoe and tissue paper`
- `the sole edge appears briefly without distortion`
- `[one verified visible detail] is shown once, then held still`

## 通勤单鞋导入动作

- `holds one shoe while wearing the matching shoe on the other foot`
- `takes exactly three relaxed steps across the office lobby`
- `shifts her weight once in a floor-mirror reflection`
- `turns one ankle, taps the floor once, then holds still`
- `rotates the shoe once beside her face in natural window light`
- `tilts the shoe to reveal visible stitching and heel profile`

## 动作限制块

```text
One continuous take. One person, one pair of shoes, one primary action and one clear product moment. Natural anatomy, realistic gait and ordinary pace.
```
